Who We Are

Architecting the Benchmark for Executive Engagement

Headquartered in Dubai with a comprehensive regional operations hub in India, Influence Exchange Group specializes in closed-door conclaves, multi-track summits, and invitation-only CXO forums.

We bridge the gap between enterprise technology providers, sovereign wealth leaders, and board-level executives across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and India.

Our Vision

To be the premier global platform for executive dialogue, cross-border investment, and enterprise innovation.

Our Mission

To deliver benchmark business summits and closed-door conclaves that accelerate strategic market expansion.
